## Global kitchen controls
- Hand hygiene: wash hands before food handling, after raw produce/dirty equipment/waste, and when changing tasks; use clean protective clothing and hair restraint.
- Receiving: inspect supplier, packaging, dates/lots, temperature where applicable, damage and spoilage; reject unsafe or undocumented deliveries; record lot, quantity, received date and expiry/use-by.
- Storage: segregate raw, ready-to-eat and allergen-containing foods; cover and label with item/lot/opened or prepared time/use-by; store off the floor; follow validated temperature logs and applicable requirements.
- Temperature: use a calibrated probe and validated HACCP/FSSAI controls. The source manual states conservative targets of chilled high-risk foods <=5°C and hot-held foods >=60°C; verify current local requirements before launch.
- Prepared components must be produced and deducted through their own batch recipes. Do not deduct raw inputs again at dish assembly.
- Only chef-approved ACTIVE recipe versions may trigger chatbot stock deductions. Plain dish-name questions must not deduct stock.
- Use FEFO for eligible lots. Never silently allow negative stock; correct errors with compensating movements.
